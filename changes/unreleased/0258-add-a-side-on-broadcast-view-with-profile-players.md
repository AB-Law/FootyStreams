The viewer opens in a perspective side view of the pitch (stands, boards, striped grass, markings, goals, depth-sorted players and ball, a camera that pans along the pitch) with side-on players that face the way they run. The sim's skill moves are played as animations: step-over, drag-back, cut inside, nutmeg, roulette, rainbow flick and knock-past each move the player and the ball their own way. The top-down view stays behind a button.
