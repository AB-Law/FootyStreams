The generator gives one club in five a random manager style for drama. In the default world that made the strongest club a low-block side and the second strongest a direct one, so tactics beat player quality: with identical tactics the strongest club took 19.5 shots to the weakest's 6.4, with its own it took 8. Giants and challengers now draw their mismatch from possession, gegen-press, high-press and wing-play only (archetype key mismatch_styles); the default world is regenerated.
