summary: Play tackles, skill moves, headers and free kicks as scenes in the hi-res broadcast view
---
The broadcast view is drawn at twice the resolution with articulated players (hair, face, beard, kit, a black-and-yellow referee) and has a Names button for name tags (the player on the ball, everyone, or nobody). Dribbles, tackles, fouls, headers and free kicks in the log are played as choreographed scenes on the real players: skill moves, a take-on that ends in a tackle, a slide, a foul or a heavy touch, a header and a free kick with a wall, with either foot from the player's recorded foot. A scene is planned from where the replay really has the two players: a defender too far off is left out, one behind the carrier chases him from behind, and nobody runs through the carrier, dashes across the pitch or leaves the ball stranded.
