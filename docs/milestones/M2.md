## Verification

- `uv run check` green after every commit; `uv run check --tier pr` green (coverage floor 90%); fast tier about 39 s, PR tier about 2 min.

## Deviations from the design

1. **`WorldRng` in `domain/rng.py`** instead of `SimRng`: `sim/` is track A's and not on this branch. Same interface (`u`, `u_int`, `gauss`, `choice_weighted`, `fork`), SplitMix64, integer maths only. To unify later, alias one to the other.
2. **New domain models and a schema bump** (see above) because persistence and league may import only `domain`.
3. **`IdMint`, `squad_strength`, `textfold`** also live in `domain/` for the same layering reason.
4. **No `contracts.json`**: contracts are embedded in player/manager/staff rows, as in the M1 models. `seasons.json` has the first season but **no fixtures**: the schedule is built in M9, so the schedule coherence check (05 section 5 item 9) and W12 move to M9.
5. **No SimConfig skeleton** in `data/static`: SimConfig is track A's (M4).
6. **Denylist has about 680 entries**, not about 1,000. It is a safety net behind synthetic grammars.
7. **Nationality**: the 70/30 home/foreign split holds for youth and free agents; senior squads draw 10-13 Valmerians and the rest from 3-5 foreign nations, because the design also demands no nation above 55%.
8. **Relationship count** is about 270 for 8 clubs (design said about 600): the M1 `Relationship` model is smaller than the design table.
9. **Catchphrases** are plain text (the M1 media model has no trigger tags to validate); pronunciation `ipa` is `None`, respellings are present.
10. **Wage fitting** scales every wage of a club by one factor instead of re-rolling overpaid depth players.
11. **Archetype is not stored on `Club`**, so `verify_world` checks the structural league shape (gap, spacing) and the per-archetype bands are asserted in the generator tests.
12. "Exactly one captain candidate" is dropped: there is no captain field; the lineup AI (M9) picks the captain.
13. Several commits exceed 400 changed lines (names about 700, player generator about 700, club generator about 1,000, verify about 1,000 including tests); splitting would have left intermediate commits without their tests.
