## Design in one paragraph

Each window day a few clubs with a need (a thin position group, an ageing starter, an upgrade) shortlist players they can see (noisy scouting), pick by desire, open below their reservation price and negotiate the fee for at most three rounds against the seller's price; the player must also agree (the same willingness rule as a renewal: wage, role, club standing, ambition, loyalty). A medical can fail; an unaffordable bid is logged and dropped. A completed deal is atomic: contract, squad entry, buyer and seller ledger legs (equal and opposite, checked by L06), rows for the history, a public event with the fee and value. The outside world is one reserved club (`clb_outside`) so fees to and from abroad are mirrored ledger legs and money stays a closed system; it supplies free agents and bids on listed players. The last window day runs a squad fill so every club ends legal. Everything is a pure function from loaded state to one `WorldDelta` per day; stages are idempotent and ids are derived from the deal.

## Deviations from the design

1. **The outside world is a real `Club` row** (`clb_outside`, created lazily), not a special case; league loops exclude it. It keeps the ledger closed.
2. **Deals complete within one day**; there are no pending offers across days and no installment fees (lump sum at signing).
3. **Contract renewals happen at the rollover (1 June)**; contracts end on 30 June and `ContractExpiryStage` runs the day after the contract-end day.
4. **Unaffordable bids are logged** as rejected `Bid` rows rather than skipped silently.
5. **Squads are filled on the last window day** by promoting prospects or signing free agents at wages scaled to the club's wage budget; no emergency loans.
6. **Loans, release clauses, agents, sell-on fees and player-initiated transfer requests** are out of scope.
7. Transfer budgets are the bank share plus a share of estimated income (`transfer_budget_income_share`), so poor clubs can trade.

## Not verified / known issues

- **Economy bands are not enforced by a test.** The market narrows but does not close the gap between rich and poor clubs; see the calibration notes below.
- A 4-club 20-season run takes about 5 minutes; an eight-club season about 40 s with SQLite writes dominating.
- Mean age sits at the low end of the target. No AI position retraining. `EventSimulator` is still only a thin adapter.
