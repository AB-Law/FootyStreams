## Design in one paragraph

Pure stages read the repositories and return a `WorldDelta`; the runner applies each in its own transaction and logs
`(date, stage, delta_hash)`. Nothing in `league/` writes or reads the clock: time is `world_meta.current_date`, advanced only
by `WorldClock`. Ids are derived from what a row is about (`derive_id`), randomness comes from `WorldRng` forks keyed by
stage/match/player so adding a player or a stage never shifts anyone else's draws. A match is: load both sides from the
repositories -> weather and crowd -> `build_match_setup` (frozen sheets with resolved mood) -> `MatchSimulator` -> `derive_world_delta` -> apply.
The simulator is the only seam to Track A; today it is the result-only simulator.

## Verification

- Mood: purity and hard caps (hypothesis over any modifier set), private modifiers never in the event log, a starter in a bad mood costs 0.04-0.10 expected goal difference (matched control on `expected_goals`), replaying a stored match reproduces its log digest.
- Finance: `balance == opening + sum(ledger)` on every club after a season (verify L01), exact apportioning of prize money (hypothesis), 100% coverage on `finance.py`, `ledger.py`, `mood.py`, `standings.py`, `verify/league.py`.
- Season: every fixture played once, standings snapshot per matchday, **replay determinism** (same seed twice -> identical standings and ledger/match-digest fingerprint), **SQLite backend gives the identical season** as memory, a stopped SQLite run resumes to the same table, a logged day re-run changes nothing, memory tables stay empty.

## Deviations from the design

1. **Matchday spacing is 21 days, not 7**, so 14 matchdays plus the off-season fit one in-world year (07 section 1 assumed weekly play). Consequence: fatigue from a match is gone long before the next one, so rotation is exercised by unit tests and by injuries, not by fixture congestion.
2. **Life events are rolled weekly** (on pay day) with the chance of at least one event in the window, not daily per player, to keep the daily tick cheap (a daily full read of 260 players dominated run time). Recovery reads everyone only on pay day and otherwise only the injured and squads that played in the last 6 days; the outcome is identical.
3. **`ticket_price_scale` (2.5) in `league.yaml`** sizes gate income to about 30% of a club's income without regenerating the seeded worlds; `ticket_price_base` stays the club's relative price point.
4. **Prize pool is a share of the league's broadcast income** (`prize_pool_share_of_broadcast`), not of "total yearly income", because income is not stored on a club.
5. **Season stats are not a table**: appearances and goals go to the open `CareerStint`; per-season totals are derived from stored summaries. A player table for season stats stays with M10.
6. **`EventSimulator` is a thin adapter** around an injected `run_match` and is exercised only with a fake callable; the real wiring belongs to the merge of the two tracks.
7. **No kickoff preamble yet**: public storylines travel in `PlayerSnapshot.public_storylines`; the `PlayerPreamble` event belongs to Track A.
8. **The 20-season statistical check** listed under M9 needs season rollover and is done in M10 (`league --seasons N`).
9. **Referee choice** is a seeded pick per match, not a rotation with fatigue/assignment rules.
10. **Commits 12 and 14 exceed 400 lines** (the daily tick, season runner and their acceptance tests only make sense together).

## Not verified / known issues

- Economy over many seasons: one club in the seeded 8-club world loses about 20m a year (an archetype that overspends on purpose). Board reactions, wage cuts and sales are M10/M11; the 20-season economy band check is M10.
- A full 8-club season takes about 11 s in memory and about 4x that on SQLite (row writes dominate); fine for now, revisit before the 20-season soak.
- Suspended players picked only when fewer than 11 are fit are not marked as having served their ban.
