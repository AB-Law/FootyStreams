## Design in one paragraph

Season-end progression moves every attribute by an expected number of points (age curve for its group x room to grow below potential x training x playing time x character), rounded to whole points by a seeded chance; gains are trimmed one point at a time until ability fits under potential, and every point is journaled with its cause. Weekly training adds a third of a year's growth in single points. The rollover is one pure function from the finished season's loaded state to one `WorldDelta`: awards, club year-end, retirements, contract renewals scaled to the wage budget, progression with the real minutes share, academy intake (players created by the injected `ProspectFactory`, ids from the request key), free-agent pool top-up and trim, squad rebalance, the next season. Intake and journeymen are scaled from the level the league had at its first rollover (kept in `world_meta`) so the league cannot feed back on itself.

## Verification

- Development: age-curve shape (teenagers grow, veterans decline, physical declines before mental, physical growth peaks before technical), `CA <= PA` always (hypothesis), the journal explains every attribute delta (hypothesis), better training and more games grow faster, micro-steps bounded.
- Retirement: chance rises with age and falls with ability; youth never retires; deterministic per player.
- Rollover (four-club league): next season created with next year's dates, valid players, retirees have no club, 22-28 seniors and 2 keepers per club, squad entries equal the contracted players with unique numbers, nobody on an expired contract, unique names, awards and retirements in the feed, new budgets, journal bounded; **same seed twice gives an identical world after two seasons** (standings, ledger, matches and every player).
- Statistical (slow, `statistical`): 20 seasons of a four-club league keep league ability within 1.5 of the start, mean age in 24-28.5, every club's squad legal at every season end, natural retirements 3-10% of seniors a year (`tests/unit/league/test_long_run.py`).
- Regression `0086`: academy intake is the requested age on the rollover day.

## Deviations from the design

1. **Weekly micro-steps are single-point gains drawn by chance**, not fractional "progress points" stored per attribute (the Player model has nowhere to keep them, and a schema change was not worth it); expected values match.
2. **No AI position retraining**: the code path for `TrainingFocus.POSITION_RETRAIN` is not exercised; nothing sets it. Position competence only changes when prospects are promoted (main position set to the natural level).
3. **Contract renewal is automatic** (wage between the old wage and the market wage, scaled to the club's wage budget); real negotiation, expiry and free-agent departures are M11.
4. **Free agents leave the game** (`left_the_game` event) when the pool exceeds 40; the design's "pool persists" is kept for the best 40.
5. **Squad rules are enforced by the rollover only**; during a season injuries can leave a club short (the lineup AI then fields injured players rather than fail).
6. **Season history / `SeasonRecord`** is carried by `WorldEvent`s (champion, awards) and the standings snapshots; no separate record table.
7. **Youth matches and loans** remain out of scope.
8. The rollover runs on the day after the season ends (1 June), before the 30 June contract end and the summer window (M11).

## Not verified / known issues

- **Economy**: league wage/income bands are not enforced by a test. In the eight-club world the rich clubs accumulate cash (about +50m a year for the richest) and the three poorest run balances that stay overdrawn (-30m to -60m after ten years, improving slowly). Financial sales and the transfer market that spends the accumulated cash are M11.
- A 4-club 20-season run takes about 5 minutes; an eight-club season about 40 s with SQLite writes dominating.
- Mean age sits at the low end of the target (25-26 against 25.5-27.5).
