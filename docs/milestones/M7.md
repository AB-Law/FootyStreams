## Deviations from the design

1. **Schema was extended.** The brief says not to change frozen M1 contracts unless a field truly must; the summary's maps, injuries, frames and tags cannot exist without these fields. They are additive with defaults and in their own commit.
2. **Why SIM_VERSION moves in the schema commit.** `kickoff` records `SCHEMA_VERSION`, so every golden digest changes with the schema bump; the 0.4.0 bump is made there, and the enabling commit re-pins goldens from a deleted file (`golden update` refuses a rewrite under an unchanged version). One bump for the milestone.
3. **Fold lives in `events/derive`**, not `sim/summary`, because `verify` and `analytics` may not import `sim`. The xT surface moved there too; `sim/threat.py` re-exports it.
4. **Frames** use the engine's own position steps (linear interpolation); no `shape` metrics or `phase`. They take sequence numbers, so ids shift; the non-frame events are identical after renumbering. The digest covers frames.
5. **Not in the summary**: the lists `goals`/`cards`/`substitutions`/`tactical_changes`/`formations_used` (scan the log), `second_assist_id`, per-event `kind` enums.
6. **Tags** the log cannot support are not emitted (see section 16 notes). `high_stakes` and friends wait for league context.
7. **Position of a row** is the best-competence position; goals conceded are not credited to an emergency keeper.
8. **Hooks** needing league context (upset, debut goal, unbeaten run) are absent.
9. **Ratings** have no "error leading to a goal" or psxg terms.
10. **Fast tier** is now about 75 s because every `verify_match` also re-validates every event (M13) and rebuilds the summary (M14).
